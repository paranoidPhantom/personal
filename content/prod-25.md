## Returning to the finals

My award from the previous year qualified me directly for the finals, which consisted of an individual project and a team hackathon.

### Individual project

The assignment was to build a fitness tracker that helped users plan workouts and stay motivated. I built a mobile-first web app with Nuxt 3 and shadcn/vue and received 73 points for the individual stage.

[View the project on GitHub](https://github.com/paranoidPhantom/prod-individual-25).

### Team hackathon

![Venue for the PROD finals](/_images/prod-25/location.png)

The team stage was a four-day, in-person hackathon with several briefs to choose from. I was the team lead and one of three frontend developers, working alongside two backend developers.

We chose to build a book-exchange service. The idea was to let people leave books they no longer needed at designated locations, where other readers could pick them up.

We received 80 points for the team stage.

## The result

The final ranking combined scores from the individual project and team hackathon. I received a bronze medal.
