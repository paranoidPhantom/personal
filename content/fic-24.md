## The challenge

The challenge was to create a 3D map of underground utility networks. This involved drawing cylinders between coordinate points, color-coding them to convey information, and storing them in a database so users could build the map collaboratively.

> In this context, utility networks include power lines, water pipes, sewage systems, and similar infrastructure.

## Technical tradeoffs

We chose [Cesium.js](https://cesium.com/) to fit the hackathon's time constraints. An alternative like [Three.js](https://threejs.org) would have required more implementation time, though I felt it offered a more maintainable foundation and would be easier to work with in the long run.

We delivered a prototype within the deadline, though it still needed further development before it could become a finished product.
